My life

This page is a snapshot of how I spend my days, what keeps my head spinning, and the little routines that help me stay grounded. I’m someone who enjoys both quiet focus and the thrill of a last-minute problem chase.

Daily rhythm

Mornings usually start with coffee and a quick review of yesterday’s notes. Afternoon work is split between school, contest practice, and robot build sessions. Evenings are when I read, sketch ideas, or catch up with friends online.

What keeps me busy

  • School and robotics take up a lot of my weekday energy.
  • I make time for algorithms, music, and drawing when I can.
  • I like planning new systems more than completing the first draft.

Milestones

  • Built my first program at age 10.
  • Spent 300+ hours in robotics practice last year.
  • Learned to fix a broken robot motor at 2 a.m.

Moments

I remember the first time I solved a problem that felt impossible — it was the moment I felt something click. I also appreciate the calm of an evening when the world quiets and I can finally enjoy the results of a day’s work.

More on the side

Planning

I enjoy setting up my time so I can balance school, contests, and creative projects without burning out.

Learning

I’m always picking up new skills, whether it’s a programming technique, a mechanical trick, or a new way to organize my day.
